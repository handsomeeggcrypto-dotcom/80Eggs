Plug in 🥚🎸

  1. Headphones go in the M-Audio's headphone jack. Not speakers, so it can't squeal.
  2. Push the M-Audio's Direct Monitor button up (Off), or you'll hear the plain sound on top.
  3. Guitar or pickup goes in the guitar jack. The yellow SD-1 can go in between.
  4. Plug the MiniLab and the ControlPad into USB.
  5. Press START. Chrome asks to use the microphone: that's the M-Audio. Say yes.

SOUNDS:
Knobs: drag up/down, scroll, or double-click to reset. MiniLab: turn its knobs one at a time and each one grabs the next knob here, left to right. Its keys play the PITCH pedal: hold a key and your sound jumps to that note (middle C = no change). ControlPad: hit the pads left to right and they become the footswitches (pad 7 = record, pad 8 = silence). To change what a knob or pad does, press LEARN, click the thing on screen, then move the knob or hit the pad. Keyboard: 1–6 stomp the pedals.
Try: plug a spare pickup in and hold it near your phone, a charger or the laptop, then press GHOST RADIO.
